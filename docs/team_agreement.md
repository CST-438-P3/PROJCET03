# Team Agreement: Team 1 (Furever Near)

Change this file only through a pull request. Our PR reports link to this file as it stood that
sprint (open it on GitHub, press `y`, and use that permalink).

## PR deadline

The course deadline is the outer limit: PR open by **2:00 AM Thursday**, reviewed and merged by
**10:00 AM Thursday**. Our own deadline is earlier:

- **Our PRs are open by:** Wednesday 10:00 PM
- **Why that time works for us:** It leaves time for reviews and for the captain to merge before the course deadline.
- **If you will be late:** tell the team in Slack before 10:00 PM.

## Reviews

- **A requested reviewer responds within:** 2 hours
- **A PR that needs changes is updated within:** 12 hours

## Communication

- **Team channel:** Slack
- **How someone says they are out** (so the deputy can merge): Post in the team Slack channel before 10:00 PM on Wednesday of that sprint.
